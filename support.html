Frequently asked questions

How do I send an invitation?

Pick an occasion and a design, fill in the details and tap the button to send it. Glimi creates a link that you can send by text message, WhatsApp or any app in your share menu, or copy and paste anywhere.

Do my guests need the app?

No. Guests open the link in their browser and answer "I'll be there" or "Can't make it". No app and no sign-up are needed.

Where do I see who is coming?

Open My events and tap an invitation to see how many guests are going and how many can't make it. Glimi shows the number of answers, not guest names.

How do I delete an invitation?

Open My events, tap the invitation and tap Delete invite. After that, guests can no longer open its link. If you no longer have the app on the device you made the invitation on, email us its link and we will delete it.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Glimi Pro screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Glimi Pro.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Glimi and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and has no subscription to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
