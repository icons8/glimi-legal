Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Glimi app ("Glimi", "we", "us") and its invitation pages collect, why, and what choices you have. Glimi is provided by Generated Media, Inc.

The short version

  • Glimi has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The invitation you publish (its text and image) is stored on our servers so your guests can open it by link. Anyone who has the link can see it.
  • Guests only tell us "going" or "not going". We do not ask guests for their names or contacts.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. We do not use the advertising identifier, do not show ads and do not sell your data.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete an invitation in the app at any time.

Information we collect

Invitations you publish

When you publish an invitation, we store what you entered in it - for example the event title, date and time, place or address, host name - together with the design choices (occasion, layout, font, colors) and the invitation image, which the app creates on your device from your design. We need this to show the invitation page to your guests. Please do not put information in an invitation that you do not want the people you share it with to see.

The invitation page is available to anyone who has its link. The link contains a randomly generated code, and the page asks search engines not to index it. When you share the link in a messaging app (for example iMessage, WhatsApp or Telegram), that service may load the page to show a preview with the title, details and image.

A random device identifier

On first launch the app creates a random identifier and keeps it on your device. The app sends it to our servers so that only this device can edit or delete the invitations it created. We do not link it to your name or contact details. When you make a purchase, the app also passes this identifier to Apple as part of the purchase. If you delete the app, the identifier is lost and the app can no longer manage your past invitations; you can ask us to delete them (see "Your choices").

Guest answers

When a guest answers an invitation, we store only whether they are going, as a count shown to the host. To stop the same browser from answering twice, the page saves the guest's answer in a cookie (kept for up to one year) and in the browser's local storage. We do not ask guests for any personal information.

Usage analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how features are used: for example, that an invitation was opened in the editor, created or shared and through which channel, that the paywall was shown or a trial started. These events do not contain the text of your invitations. Firebase also collects standard technical information, such as an app instance identifier, the identifier for vendor (IDFV), device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate location derived from your IP address, and it automatically records sessions, screens viewed and in-app purchases. Ad personalization signals are turned off, and the app does not access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this data to provide the analytics service to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status on your device. Firebase Analytics records that a purchase or trial happened, as described above.

IP addresses and technical data

Like any online service, our servers and providers receive your device's IP address and basic request information when the app or an invitation page connects to them. We may use this information to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors, including through an error and performance monitoring service. We do not use it to build profiles about you.

How we use information

  • To store and show your invitations to the people you share them with.
  • To count guest answers and show them to the host.
  • To understand and improve how the app is used.
  • To provide paid features you bought.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers

We use providers that process data on our behalf: cloud hosting and storage for invitations and images, Google Firebase for analytics, an error and performance monitoring service for our servers, and Apple for purchases. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep information

Invitations and their images are kept until you delete them in the app or ask us to delete them. When an invitation is deleted, we remove it from our active systems; copies that messaging apps or browsers have already saved, or that remain in caches and backups for a limited time, may persist for a while. Analytics data is kept according to the data retention setting of our Google Analytics property. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Your choices

  • Delete an invitation in the app at any time.
  • If you can no longer manage an invitation in the app (for example, after deleting the app), email us its link at support@icons8.com and we will delete it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to certain processing. Contact us to use them; we will respond within the time required by law.

Children

Glimi is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

International transfers

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to use your rights, contact Generated Media, Inc. at support@icons8.com.
